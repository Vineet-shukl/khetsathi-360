## 5. Mathematical & Agronomic Formulations Reference

The presentation incorporates deterministic scientific formulas implemented across `app/core/agronomics.py`, `app/core/soil_baseline.py`, and `app/core/weather_service.py`:

### 5.1 DAP Nitrogen Credit Offset Formula
When a farmer applies Di-Ammonium Phosphate (DAP) for Phosphorus ($P_2O_5$), it simultaneously delivers 18% elemental Nitrogen ($N$). To prevent chemical fertilizer burn, KhetSathi 360 automatically offsets the required Urea top-dress:

$$DAP_{bags} = \left\lceil \frac{Target\,P_2O_5\,(kg/ha) \times Area\,(ha)}{0.46 \times 50\,kg} \right\rceil$$
$$N_{from\,DAP} = DAP_{bags} \times 50\,kg \times 0.18$$
$$N_{remaining} = \max\left(0, Target\,N\,(kg/ha) \times Area\,(ha) - N_{from\,DAP}\right)$$
$$Urea_{bags} = \left\lceil \frac{N_{remaining}}{0.46 \times 45\,kg} \right\rceil$$

### 5.2 Spatial Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW) Baseline Formula
For farmers without an official Soil Health Card, expected N-P-K and pH are computed from centroids across 695 rural districts using 2nd-degree inverse distance weighting:

$$\hat{z}(x) = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{k} w_i z_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{k} w_i} \quad \text{where} \quad w_i = \frac{1}{d(x, x_i)^2}$$

### 5.3 FAO-56 Reference Evapotranspiration ($ET_0$)
Calculates daily crop water demand in mm/day from Open-Meteo temperature, solar radiation, wind speed, and relative humidity:

$$ET_0 = \frac{0.408 \Delta (R_n - G) + \gamma \frac{900}{T + 273} u_2 (e_s - e_a)}{\Delta + \gamma (1 + 0.34 u_2)}$$
